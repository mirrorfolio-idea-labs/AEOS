## Retrospective

**What went right:** building on the staged `memory.propose` seam meant
apply mode was mostly mapping — atomicity, budget checks, index sync, and
the audit hook were inherited, not rebuilt. Pure-function eligibility made
the idle trigger testable without fake timers. The RED phase caught two
real design questions early (index-vs-content in the never-delete proof;
summarizer shrinkage vs budgets).

**What to watch:** the extractive summarizer is deliberately crude — it
halves each source; real curation quality arrives with the P3.M2-routed
model summarizer behind the `summarize` seam. The daemon still runs
dry-run-only (see scope note) — memory curation changes nothing in
production until an apply trigger is approved. And P2.M5 needs `node-pty`
+ `@xterm/*`: new third-party dependencies the overnight stop conditions
forbid installing without Kabeer's say-so.
