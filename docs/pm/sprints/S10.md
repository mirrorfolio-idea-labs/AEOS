## Retrospective

**What went right:** recording fixtures BEFORE writing the translator meant
every design question was answered by ground truth (resume re-announces a
new thread id; turn numbering is meaningless per-line). The opencode mirror
made conformance parity nearly free.

**What to watch:** codex cost fidelity is tokens-only — USD stays unset
rather than fabricated; the P3.M2 pricing router can derive it later.
Canonical turn.* events are deliberately unemitted by both real adapters;
if a consumer ever needs them, the taxonomy payload should drop the
mandatory counter first. And `.claude-flow/` tooling litter is now
gitignored after it snuck into the T1 commit (amended out).
